The benchmark sampled 26 current sites. Company homepages were checked live at desktop width. The portfolio set was checked through its live site where it loaded and through its Awwwards project preview and description; some heavily animated personal sites remained on an intro or failed to load in the automated browser, so their deeper interaction behavior is not treated as observed evidence. ### Synthesis

The strongest transferable pattern is **identity beside immediate evidence**: a compact introduction, then an unmistakably real product surface with enough context to understand it. Typography should control reading order rather than dominate the viewport. Project imagery should have different crops and proportions based on the product. Navigation should be plain. Motion should reveal a state change, and it must stop for reduced-motion users. The result should use Ajmir's own systems, screenshots, and photograph; it should not borrow another site's graphic language.

## Previous frontend audit

The prior home began with a very large two-line name, a text block and side project index, followed by a dark Azaeron section, repeated project rows, a six-card engineering grid, and a large dark footer. At 390 and 1440 px the product evidence appeared after substantial text. The Work page repeated editorial rows and then switched to smaller development cards. Engineering presented ten equal bordered cards, flattening proof and making the page resemble a résumé. Case pages had useful factual detail but small screenshots and a documentation-like sidebar; the same framing repeated across products. Notes had one entry and a disproportionate footer. About used a real photograph but repeated the very large page heading. The contact form and print résumé worked, yet looked like separate sections of the old visual grammar.

The 15 public routes, 404, menu, themes, form and print résumé were inspected through existing 390/1440 light/dark captures and source. The old design review was supplemented by the new 14-width responsive sweep. The existing evidence and maturity labels remain the source of truth.

## Identity and design direction

The new mark derives from the repeated **A** in Ajmir Aribam: two open ascenders joined by one crossbar. It is a letter construction, without a surrounding container. The custom-spaced uppercase wordmark complements it; the full name remains readable text in navigation and résumé contexts. The two ascenders also suggest connected parts of one system. The mark is a vector in the header, footer, favicon and social preview, with raster app icons generated from that source.

The brand uses warm paper (`#f2f0e9`), deep mineral green (`#172820`) and an oxide accent (`#954329`); the dark theme uses its own green surfaces, pale text and lighter accent. The oxide is for labels and interaction emphasis, while green carries the main hierarchy. Each project keeps its own real image colors. Rules organize content more often than boxes, and corners stay restrained. Contrast was checked by axe in both themes across every public route.

Instrument Sans is loaded locally as one licensed variable font (about 30 KB transferred). It supplies the display, heading and body hierarchy; system monospace is reserved for a few indices. The largest heading stops at roughly 75 px on wide desktop, while paragraph measures and line height carry the reading experience. The type remains recognizable without the accent color.

The layout has a 1376 px maximum outer shell, a narrower prose measure and a small spacing scale. Home pairs a concise product claim with three real product surfaces in the opening; its later sections change composition according to the project. Work gives Azaeron a lead treatment, SHAPES and Friends distinct rows, and the in-development systems a smaller but legible section. Its data record supports maturity, audience, role, technical focus, media, public repository when verified, limitations and feature flags, so additional projects can be indexed without duplicating page markup. Labs keeps the two prototypes separate.

Case studies open with authentic media, an audience and scope summary, then a decision or workflow that makes the engineering visible. Azaeron uses the public sign-in and documentation surfaces because its authenticated production workspace was not independently inspected. SHAPES combines public pages with a publishing-state flow. Friends uses several genuine fabrication photographs and explains the inquiry path. Verity and The Scent Bar show implemented scope and explicit release gaps. Engineering connects four system layers to project decisions and gives quality engineering a separate, concrete section. The one-entry Notes index is treated as a focused article feature, with topic, duration and related systems for future growth.

Motion is limited to navigation, link and image-state feedback through short transforms; there is no scroll control. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes those transitions. On narrow screens, the opening stacks, project media moves ahead of supporting copy, case navigation becomes an inline link strip and the menu uses ordinary links with Escape handling. At 768 px the two-column arrangements that still have enough room stay intact; smaller widths use a single reading path. The footer remains proportional on all routes.

Visitor copy names the person, product, action and current state before technology. No usage, revenue, uptime, conversion or AI-accuracy figures were invented. Exact contribution boundaries that still need confirmation remain in source as `TODO_OWNER_VERIFY`, without appearing on public pages. The original Git remotes were not exposed as links because unauthenticated access returned 404 during the evidence pass.

## Verification record — 24 September 2026

Full-page captures of all 15 routes at 390, 768, 1440 and 1728 px in light and dark themes were reviewed locally. Home, Work, Azaeron, Engineering, About, Contact and the résumé received closer composition review; the other case studies, Labs, Notes and Privacy were checked for consistency, cropping and reading order. The generated screenshots and résumé PDF are ignored test artifacts. The 14-width sweep from 320 to 1920 px found no horizontal overflow on any route. The print résumé rendered as a single A4 page with selectable text, clear experience/project separation and no clipping.

`make verify` passed on the rebuilt candidate. It includes formatting, lint, strict TypeScript, two frontend unit tests, four backend unit/API tests, a fresh PostgreSQL migration and persistence integration test, a production build, seven Playwright tests, dependency audits, committed/staged secret scans, frontend/backend Docker builds, isolated Compose startup and inquiry persistence, foreign-origin rejection, and `git diff --check`. Browser tests covered 15 route responses and links, metadata, images, navigation, keyboard menu and anchors, contact success/fallback, 404, theme behavior and absence of console/hydration errors. Axe found zero automated WCAG A/AA violations across all 15 routes in light and dark themes. The existing local Compose stack was rebuilt and is healthy at `http://localhost:3000`.
